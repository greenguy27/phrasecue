How to use it

1. Sync once per lyrics file. Press S. The full mix plays. Tap Space each time a blue-outlined line starts. Made a mistake? Backspace rewinds and lets you retap, or click any line to restart syncing from that line.
2. Sing. Press S again to leave sync mode, press V for the instrumental, then Space to play.
3. Keep it. Timings auto-save in this browser. Use Timing ▾ → 💾 Save timing file to put a file next to the song; drop that file in on the other computer.
4. Practice. Loop a line or section, slow it down, and record yourself over the instrumental. With both mixes loaded, 🧲 Snap nudges your taps onto the real vocal starts, shown as blue ticks on the timeline.

🎤 PhraseCue

A lyric prompter for singers. Load your full mix, your instrumental and your lyrics, tap once through the song to time it, then sing along with each line lighting up on cue — including your vocal-phrasing notes: stress marks, pitch arrows, breath marks and performance directions.

Private by design. PhraseCue runs entirely in your browser. Your audio and lyrics are never uploaded, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking. Timings are saved in your browser and in timing files you download.
