Lantern Road

[Style, Genre, Type: Dark Folk, Americana, Duet]
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
[Male vocal, low and close]
The lantern swings on a broken nail
Shadows walk where the fences fail
(walk on, walk on)

[Female vocal, restrained]
Frost on the window, ash in the hearth
I kept your seat at the end of the dark

[Pre-Chorus]
[Male vocal]
And every mile I swear I hear your name (your name)

[Chorus]
[Both vocals]
Lantern road, carry me home
Through the hollow and over the stone
Leave the light where the river bends
Lantern road, bring me back again

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 2]
[Female vocal]
I counted stars till the morning came
[Male vocal]
I counted miles and they're all the same

[Final Chorus]
[Both vocals]
Lantern road, carry me home
Through the hollow and over the stone

[Outro]
[Female vocal, softly]
Lantern road
