# PAPER BOATS
### Vocal Phrasing Guide (example)
*Sample song written for the PhraseCue demo · Folk Pop · ~84 BPM*

---

## [VERSE 1]
*(Soft and close to the mic.)*

→ We folded **PA-per** / ↘ on the kitchen floor,
→ wrote our names / on the **SAILS——** ↘

↗ Sent them down / the gutter **STREAM——**
↘ like they'd find / a world with **TRAILS——** ~

//

---

## [CHORUS]
*(Lift. Let the vowels ring.)*

↗ **SAIL** on, / little **BOAT——** ↗
→ carry what / we couldn't **SAY——**

↗ If the river / keeps you **FLOAT-ing** ↗
↘ we'll be fine / some other **DAY——** ~

//

---

### [OUTRO]
*(Fade, almost spoken.)*

↘ Sail on——
